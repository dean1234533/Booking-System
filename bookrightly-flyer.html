No card needed

In-person pitch — 45 seconds

For handing the flyer to a business owner face to face. Say it like you mean it, not like you're reading it — the beats matter more than the exact words.

Open
"Hey — sorry to interrupt, quick one. Do you take bookings by phone or WhatsApp at the moment?"
Let them answer. Almost every trade says yes — that's the opening.
Problem
"That's exactly who I built this for — texting back and forth, chasing no-shows, losing bookings when you're mid-appointment and can't answer the phone."
Solution
"It's called Bookrightly — you get your own booking page, clients book and pay a deposit online, and it lands straight in your calendar. No more chasing."
Risk reversal
"It's free for 90 days, no card needed to start. If it's not for you, you've lost nothing. After that it's £10 to £20 a month depending on your trade."
Flexibility
"It already covers barbers, hairdressers, PTs, decorators — and if your trade's not on there, tell me and I'll get it added."
Only bring this up if their trade genuinely isn't listed — don't lead with it.
Close
"Here's a flyer with the link — or if you've got two minutes now, I can set your page up on my phone before I leave."
Offering to set it up on the spot converts far better than "check it out later." Always offer.

If they push back

I already use [Fresha / Booksy / a paper diary]
"No pressure to switch today — take the flyer, and if the fees or faff ever bother you, I'm local and easy to reach."
I don't have time right now
"Totally get it — the flyer's got the link, takes five minutes whenever suits you. No card needed to try it."
What's it cost me really?
"Free for 90 days. After that £10-20 a month depending on your trade, plus a small fee only on bookings you actually take — so it only costs you when it's working."
My trade isn't listed
"Tell me what you do — I build these myself, I can usually turn a new trade around fast."
